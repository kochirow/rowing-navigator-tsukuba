## Account and Data Deletion

Open “Privacy and Data” in the App and select “Delete account and data.”

This removes the Firebase anonymous account, team membership, real-time shared position, temporary hazards created by the user, practice records, device settings, and on-device error records. If you are the team administrator, hand the administrator role to another member first.

If you cannot open the App, request deletion by email or through the support form.

## Lost Device

If your device is lost, contact your team administrator and support as soon as possible.

- The administrator can remove the lost device's member from the member list on the App's “Team” screen. Removing a member also renews the invitation code, so the old code can no longer be used to join.
- Members are identified by their “Member ID” (the first 8 characters). Each member's ID is shown at the top of their “Team” screen. Noting it in advance makes this easier.

Do not send your invitation code, password, or API key.

## Team Administrator

The person who created the team is the first administrator. The administrator can renew the invitation code and remove members.

- **Before changing devices or leaving the club**, use “Make administrator” from the menu at the right end of the member list on the “Team” screen to hand the role to another member.
- If the administrator has left the team or deleted their account, a remaining member can become administrator with “Take over as administrator” on the “Team” screen.
- If the administrator's device is lost and the role cannot be handed over, contact support. After confirming identity, the operator removes the lost administrator from the team, and a remaining member can then use “Take over as administrator.”

## Leaked Invitation Code

The administrator can press “Renew invitation code” on the “Team” screen; the old code stops working immediately. If someone you do not recognize joined with the leaked code, remove them from the member list (this also renews the code).

If you cannot reach the administrator, contact support.

## Hazard Deleted by Mistake

A deleted temporary hazard can be restored for 7 days. Open “Recently deleted” at the top right of the hazard screen and press “Restore.” Any team member can do this, and the hazard immediately returns to every team member's map and warnings.

## Incorrect or Outdated Hazard Information

If you find an incorrect hazard, an incorrectly positioned fixed-driftwood marker, or a temporary hazard that is no longer needed, correct or delete it in the App when possible. You may also contact support. An inappropriate temporary hazard can be reported by selecting it on the hazard screen and choosing “Report inappropriate hazard.”

Please include:

- Team name.
- The approximate location of the hazard.
- What is incorrect.
- The requested correction or deletion.

Do not include invitation codes, passwords, or API keys.
