## Account and Data Deletion

Open “Privacy and Data” in the App and select “Delete account and data.”

This removes the Firebase anonymous account, team membership, real-time shared position, temporary hazards created by the user, practice records, and device settings.

If you cannot open the App, request deletion by email or through the support form.

## Lost Device

If your device is lost, contact support as soon as possible.

If available, include your team name, the display name used in the App, and the approximate time when the App was last used. Do not send your invitation code, password, or API key.

## Leaked Invitation Code

If a team invitation code may have been disclosed outside the team, contact support as soon as possible.

Depending on the situation, the current team may need to be retired and a new team with a new invitation code may need to be created.

## Incorrect or Outdated Hazard Information

If you find an incorrect hazard, an incorrectly positioned fixed-driftwood marker, or a temporary hazard that is no longer needed, correct or delete it in the App when possible. You may also contact support.

Please include:

- Team name.
- The approximate location of the hazard.
- What is incorrect.
- The requested correction or deletion.

Do not include invitation codes, passwords, or API keys.

## Safety Notice

Rowing Navigator supports navigation safety checks. It does not guarantee collision avoidance or prevent accidents.

GPS errors, communication failures, low battery, operating-system restrictions, or other conditions may delay or stop position display and warnings.
